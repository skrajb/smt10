How it works: pick a demo type, then your language. You get a full 5-minute typing test. The timer starts with your first key, and your speed and accuracy appear automatically when time is up.

Chunk Based Demo

Typing Practice · 10 chunks

5 min

The passage is split into 10 short chunks. Type one chunk, then move on to the next — the same way as the Typing Practice (Chunk) tests.

  • Enter or ↓ next chunk · ↑ previous chunk
  • Speed is counted on characters: 5 characters = 1 word

Words Based Demo

Long Paragraph · word count

5 min

The whole passage is on screen and you type it in one go, like the Long Paragraph tests. The result is calculated on the words you typed.

  • Net words = typed words − mistakes (wrong, extra, skipped words)
  • Result speed = net words ÷ minutes (PHHC / PAU style)
